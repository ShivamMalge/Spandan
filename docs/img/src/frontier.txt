OPERATING-POINT FRONTIER -- what each alert budget buys
==============================================================================
The threshold is chosen to maximise net rupees SUBJECT TO an alerts/day cap,
not to maximise net rupees outright. The unconstrained criterion was selecting
the operating point using a cost model that prices a wrongly-blocked declining
transaction at almost nothing - so it bought recall with false positives.
